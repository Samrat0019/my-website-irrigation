Irrigation Advisory Workflow Farmer registers the farm. Farm and crop information is collected. Soil moisture data is obtained from sensors. Weather conditions are analyzed. Crop growth stage is identified. AI analyzes the collected information. Irrigation requirements are calculated. Recommendation is provided to the farmer. Farmer follows the irrigation schedule. Farm conditions are continuously monitored. Data Sources Soil Moisture Sensors Weather Forecast Satellite Images Crop Information Soil Reports IoT Devices Stakeholders and Responsibilities

Farmer
