AI-Based Irrigation Advisory

System Overview

The Smart Irrigation Advisory System analyzes agricultural and environmental data to provide suitable irrigation recommendations for sugarcane farmers. Important Irrigation Recommendation Farmers should irrigate the crop according to soil moisture conditions, crop growth stage, weather forecast, and water requirements. Current Advisory: Monitor soil moisture before irrigation. Water Conservation Practices Use irrigation water efficiently and avoid unnecessary irrigation. Efficient irrigation helps reduce water wastage and supports sustainable agriculture.
