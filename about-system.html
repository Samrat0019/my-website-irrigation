About Smart Irrigation Advisory System The Smart Irrigation Advisory System is an AI-enabled agricultural platform developed to assist sugarcane farmers in making informed irrigation decisions.

About Smart Irrigation Advisory System
Objectives
Reduce unnecessary water consumption.
Provide timely irrigation recommendations.
Monitor soil moisture conditions.
Analyze weather conditions.
Improve sugarcane crop productivity.
Support data-driven agricultural practices.
Promote sustainable water management.
System Features
AI-based irrigation recommendations
Soil moisture monitoring
Weather information
Crop growth monitoring
Farm information management
IoT sensor integration
GIS and GPS-based farm visualization
Agricultural advisory services
Farmer registration
Benefits


The system helps farmers use water more efficiently and make better decisions about when and how much to irrigate. Major benefits include: Water conservation Reduced irrigation costs Better crop management Improved crop productivity Continuous farm monitoring Data-driven decision making Sustainable agricultural practices Agricultural Expert Message “Smart irrigation means providing the right amount of water at the right time while protecting our valuable water resources.”
