The Smart Irrigation Advisory System is an AI and IoT-based agricultural platform designed to help sugarcane farmers make better irrigation decisions. The system uses soil moisture, weather conditions, crop information, and farm data to provide timely irrigation recommendations.
Our Goal

To provide farmers with accurate, simple, and data-driven irrigation advice while reducing water wastage and improving sugarcane crop productivity. Key Services AI-based irrigation recommendations
Soil moisture monitoring
Weather information
Crop growth monitoring
Farm information management
Water conservation advisory
Agricultural expert support
